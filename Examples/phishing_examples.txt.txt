PHISHING EXAMPLES

CodeAlpha Cyber Security Internship – Task 2

==================================================

1. Fake Bank/Payment Alert (Email)

From: alerts@sbi-secure-verify.com

Subject: Your SBI account has been temporarily locked

"Dear Customer, unusual login detected from a new device. Verify your identity within 12 hours to avoid permanent suspension. [Verify Now]"

RED FLAGS:

• Unexpected verification request
• Urgent deadline
• Suspicious sender address
• Login link in an unexpected message
• Threat of account suspension

==================================================

## EXAMPLE 2 — FAKE PRIZE NOTIFICATION

Subject: Congratulations! You Have Won a Prize

You have been selected to receive a special reward.

Click the link below to claim your prize immediately.

RED FLAGS:

• Unexpected reward
• Pressure to act quickly
• Unknown sender
• Request for personal or payment information
• Emotionally persuasive language

==================================================

## EXAMPLE 3 — FAKE PASSWORD RESET

Subject: Password Reset Required

Your password is scheduled to expire today.

Use the link provided to keep your account active.

RED FLAGS:

• Unexpected password-reset request
• Urgent deadline
• Suspicious link
• Possible credential harvesting
• No prior request from the user

==================================================

SAFETY NOTE

These examples are completely fictional and are included
only for cybersecurity awareness and educational purposes.

Do not enter real passwords, OTPs, payment information,
or other sensitive information into suspicious websites.
